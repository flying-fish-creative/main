Our Team

Small on Purpose

Two founders. One studio. No middle-women. Just us. Thinking, making, and sweating the details on everything we touch. We started Flying Fish in the pub, like all the best ideas. Built on the belief that the best creative comes from people who are genuinely invested. In the idea, in the brand, in the outcome.

Fabiola Caceres Cardenas Experience Design Director

Faby designs the parts people actually use. She thinks in flows, systems, and the spaces between clicks, making sure every digital experience feels considered rather than cobbled together.

Rianna Woods Executive Creative Directorr

The vision person. Start to finish, brief to final build. Rianna leads the creative across every stage: strategy, concept, art direction, execution, shaping how brands look, feel, and show up in the world. The kind of CD who's in it from the first conversation to the last decision.

Marchello HR Department

Complaints, concerns, existential crises. Marchello handles them all with the same response: leaning on you until you feel better. He has never once enforced a policy. He has never needed to.

The Duo Human-Led, AI-Fluent

They've been working with AI tools long before it became everyone's personality. Midjourney, Firefly, Eleven Labs, Flow. Not as a shortcut. As a craft.

Fabiola Caceres Cardenas Journey Mapper

Someone has to figure out how a person gets from here to there without getting lost or frustrated. That's Faby. She maps the route, spots the dead ends, and makes the whole thing feel intuitive. Even when it really wasn't.

Rianna Woods Visual Storyteller

Every piece of work is telling a story. Rianna makes sure it's one worth telling. Culture-driven and POV-led, she's drawn to the stories that go untold, the ones that get overshadowed, the ones that actually have something to say. That's where the best work lives.

Marchello Chief Serotonin Officer

Marchello is a Vizsla. He has no formal qualifications but his presence in any room has been proven to reduce stress, increase morale, and occasionally derail a client call in the best possible way. Flying Fish would not function without him.

Fabiola Caceres Cardenas Inclusive Design Advocate

Good design works for everyone. Faby is a member of the BIMA Inclusive Design Council and brings that thinking into everything Flying Fish makes. Not as an afterthought. From the very beginning.

Rianna Woods Brand Strategist

The thinking before the making. Rianna brings strategic rigour to every creative decision, whether that's a brand finding its identity or a single piece of work finding its point. Nothing ships without a reason behind it.
